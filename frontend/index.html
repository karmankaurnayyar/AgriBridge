AgriBridge — Agribusiness Operations and Market Intelligence Platform

Individual internship project · Week 2: Software Architecture Foundation

AgriBridge connects farmers and buyers around farm records, crops, harvest lots and purchase requests. This Week 2 build implements Authentication and Farm Management end-to-end against a real MySQL database; Crop, Harvest Lot, Buyer Request and Matching modules are documented and schema-ready, with implementation planned for Weeks 3–4 (see docs/roadmap.md in the project repository).
